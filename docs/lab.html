EDM forecasting on a public time series

In design

What it is

Empirical Dynamic Modelling reconstructs the underlying state space of a system from a single observed time series. It does not assume a parametric model. That makes it useful for ecological data, where the dynamics are often nonlinear and the right functional form is rarely obvious in advance.

What you get to do

Pick a forecast horizon, an embedding dimension, and a train / test split. Watch the forecast track or fail to track the held out portion of the series, and read the skill metric update underneath.
